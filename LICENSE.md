## Why this license

We build pypdpg because the general public is safer when privacy-preserving
computation is ordinary: when encrypting data before it leaves your hands is a
two-line change, not a research project. That only happens if this technology
is freely available to the people protecting the public — students, teachers,
researchers, non-profits, public institutions, and the small organizations that
can't afford enterprise privacy tooling.

It also only happens if the work is sustainable. So the deal is simple: pypdpg
is free for almost everyone. If it helps power a large business, we ask that
business to buy a license — that is what funds the free part.

*This preamble explains our intent. The binding terms are below.*
